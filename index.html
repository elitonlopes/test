How to read this map

  • Each island is a part of life. Its land area shows roughly how much time went into it. Time is not a measure of skill, success or importance.

  • Dotted shoreline: the island’s size in the other perspective, so you can see what changed.

  • Hatched, dashed coast: no estimate shared. Drawn at a neutral size, not a guess.

  • Dotted outline: resting for now. Very small shares are drawn at a minimum size and marked ◦ so they stay findable.

  • Buoys offshore: what’s next, the things being worked toward.

  • Scale: the small square next to “How to read” is how much land stands for 1,000 hours (life so far) or 1 hour a week (life now).

Places on each island

Earlier experiences sit inland; recent ones sit near the shore.

    Keyboard

    Tab moves between islands, and the arrow keys jump to a neighbouring island. Enter opens an island or a place. Escape steps back. Press T to switch perspective.
