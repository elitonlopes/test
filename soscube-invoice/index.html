What the solution does

More than reading invoices

Reading text off a document is the first step. The value comes from what happens next: checking the numbers, catching what's wrong, and getting the right person to decide.

  1. 01 · Extract

    Pull out the useful fields

    Supplier, invoice number, dates, billing period, site, meter, consumption and totals, captured into one structured record.

  2. 02 · Verify

    Check values against the source

    Each value links back to where it appears on the invoice, so a reviewer can confirm it in seconds.

  3. 03 · Flag

    Catch gaps and unusual use

    Missing required information and consumption that moves outside an agreed range are raised as exceptions.

  4. 04 · Route

    Send exceptions to the right person

    An energy alert goes to whoever owns energy data. A missing site code goes to facilities. Clean invoices go straight to approval.

  5. 05 · Approve & report

    Release structured data

    Once checks are complete, the approved record is prepared in the format your reporting destination expects.

    Your invoices, your rules, your reviewers

    Smart Invoice Processing isn't a fixed product you adapt to. SOSCube implements it in your environment and configures each of these areas with your team. The right-hand column shows how the demo above is set up.

    AreaWhat can be configuredSetting in this demo

    Validation rulesRequired fields, formats, totals that must reconcile, consumption thresholds by site or utility, and comparisons with previous periods.Building code required · net + VAT = total · consumption change above ±15% vs previous period is flagged.

    About this page

    What the demo shows, and what it doesn't

    What it shows

    • How a reviewer checks extracted values against the source invoice.
    • How missing information and unusual consumption turn into assigned exceptions.
    • How approval depends on required checks being complete.
    • What a structured, approved record looks like when it's ready for reporting.

    What it doesn't do

    • It doesn't read documents. Extraction is simulated with pre-loaded values.
    • It doesn't connect to client systems, and "Reported" doesn't send anything anywhere.
    • It doesn't process or schedule payments.
    • All suppliers, sites, meters, people and amounts are fictional. The electricity invoice is a specimen inspired by a Luxembourg utility layout and is not issued by LEO.
